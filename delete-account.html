Delete your WHIZTY account

WHIZTY is an Android app that helps children aged 7 to 12 learn all the important skills like Confidence with People, Smart Money Habits, Leadership, Entrepreneurship, AI & many more. It is run by WHIZTY.

How to ask us to delete your account

  1. Open your email app and write to support@whizty.com.
  2. Send it from the email address you used to register in WHIZTY. This lets us check that it is really you.
  3. Use the subject "Delete my WHIZTY account".

You can also start this email from inside the app: tap "Delete my account (parents)" on the dashboard. In newer versions of the app, first tap the shield button on the dashboard and answer the grown-up question.

We will reply to confirm, and we will delete your account within 30 days.

What we delete

  • Your login (your email address).
  • Your full name.
  • Your consent record.
  • Your payment record (plan, amount, dates and the payment reference code).

What we may keep

  • Payment records that tax or payment rules require us to keep, for as long as the law requires. Razorpay, our payment company, keeps its own records.
  • Login-code email logs at Resend, for up to 30 days.

Your child's information

We do not hold your child's information. The nickname, age group, avatar, progress and voice recordings are only on the phone. Uninstalling the app removes them. The child can also delete voice recordings at any time from the dashboard ("Delete my voice recordings").

Leaving early access

If you emailed us asking for early access, write to support@whizty.com and we will remove you from our list and our Google Play tester list.

Questions

Write to support@whizty.com. You can also read our Privacy Policy.
