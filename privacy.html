WHIZTY Privacy Policy

Version: draft-9 · Last updated: 28 November 2026

1. Who we are

WHIZTY is an Android app that helps children aged 7 to 12 learn all the important skills like Confidence with People, Smart Money Habits, Leadership, Entrepreneurship, AI & many more, with one short mission a day. The app is run by WHIZTY.

Contact for questions, complaints and deletion requests: support@whizty.com
Complaints contact: WHIZTY, support@whizty.com

2. Who uses the app

A parent or guardian registers and agrees to this policy. The child then uses the app on the parent's phone. We do not ask the child for a real name, a birth date, a school or a location.

3. What we collect about the parent

  • Email address: to log you in with a 6-digit code. Kept in Supabase.
  • Full name: to set up your account. Kept in Supabase.
  • Consent record (yes, the time, and the version of the notice you agreed to): to show that you agreed. Kept in Supabase.
  • Mobile number (only if you choose to pay): to send you the payment link by SMS. Sent once to Razorpay. Not saved in our database.
  • Payment record (only if you pay): plan, amount, date paid, paid-until date, and a Razorpay payment reference code. Used to unlock Level 2 for the time you paid for. Kept in Supabase.

We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. They go only to Razorpay.

4. What stays only on the phone (never sent to us)

  • The child's nickname (optional), age group and avatar.
  • Lesson progress, points, streak and badges.
  • How many minutes the app was open today (for the daily rest rule).
  • The child's voice recordings.

None of this is sent to Supabase or anyone else. Android's cloud backup is switched off for the app, so it is not copied to Google Drive or to a new phone.

If you uninstall the app or change phones, this information is lost. The child can delete all voice recordings at any time from the dashboard ("Delete my voice recordings").

The app asks for the microphone only to record the child's voice for speaking missions. Recordings are stored only on the phone and are never uploaded.

5. What we do NOT do

  • No advertising, no profiling, no tracking, no analytics tools.
  • We do not use your data or your child's data for marketing.
  • We do not collect location, biometric data or photos.
  • We do not sell your data.

6. Who we share it with

We use these services to run the app and this website. They handle your data only for the reasons given:

  • Supabase: stores your account, name, consent record and payment record.
  • Resend: sends you the login code email, so it handles your email address.
  • Razorpay: takes the payment and sends you the payment link by SMS, so it handles your mobile number and payment details.
  • GitHub: hosts this website (GitHub Pages). When you visit, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details to deliver the page, under its own privacy statement.
  • Zoho: hosts our support@whizty.com mailbox, so it handles the emails you send us.
  • Google: if you email us asking for early access, we add the Gmail ID you give us to our Google Play tester list (section 13).

We may also share information if the law requires it.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account details: until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Payment records: for as long as the law requires. Tax and payment rules may require keeping some records for several years, even after you delete your account.
  • Login-code email logs at Resend: up to 30 days.
  • Everything on the phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Early access requests you email to us: until our early access programme ends, or sooner if you ask us to remove you.

8. Your rights and how to delete your data

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your parent data at any time. To delete your account and all your parent data, email support@whizty.com from the email address you registered with. You can also start this email from inside the app: tap "Delete my account (parents)" on the dashboard. In newer versions of the app, first tap the shield button on the dashboard and answer the grown-up question. We will confirm by email and delete your account within 30 days. This removes your login, your name, your consent record and your payment record from our database. Razorpay and others may keep some records as the law requires.

There is no child data on our side to delete. The child's information is on the phone, so uninstalling the app removes it.

Our account deletion page: https://whizty.com/delete-account

9. Children

The app is meant for children aged 7 to 12, used with a parent or guardian. The parent registers and agrees for the child.

10. Security

Data travels over secure connections. Each parent can read only their own rows in our database. Payment secrets are kept on our server, not in the app. No system is perfectly safe, so we cannot promise absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the version and date, and ask for your agreement again where the change needs it.

12. Contact

support@whizty.com

13. Our website and early access by email

This website has no sign-up form. It does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and it does not save anything in your browser. Our fonts are kept on our own website, so no font company receives your IP address.

To ask for early access, you can email support@whizty.com. We then have your email address, the Gmail ID you use on Google Play and anything else you write. We use them only to add you as a tester on Google Play and to write to you about early access. Google then handles your Gmail ID under its own privacy policy. We do not ask for any information about your child, so please do not include it.

To leave early access, email support@whizty.com and we will remove you from our list and our tester list.
