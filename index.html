How WHIZTY works

🎯

One mission a day

About 10 minutes. One short, doable challenge your child can finish today, not an endless feed.

🏅

Points, streaks and badges

Kids come back because it feels like a game, and every badge is a real skill practised.

🌙

A daily rest rule

The app keeps track of today's minutes on the phone, so screen time stays short and healthy.

Safety first

Why parents can trust WHIZTY

We collect as little as possible. Your child's progress never leaves your phone.

📵

No ads. No tracking.

No advertising, no profiling and no analytics tools. We never sell your data or use it for marketing.

📱

Child data stays on the phone

Nickname, age group, avatar, progress, points and badges are stored only on your phone, never on our servers.

🙈

No personal details from kids

We never ask your child for a real name, birth date, school or location. A parent sets up the account.

🎙️

Private voice practice

The microphone is used only for speaking missions. Recordings stay on the phone and can be deleted any time.

🔒

Safe payments

Paid plans go through Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

🗑️

You stay in control

See, correct or delete your parent data at any time. Account deletion is done within 30 days.

No real names needed

Your child just picks a friendly buddy to go with a nickname.
